## What Absurd does

- Multi-agent system that plans, generates and edits ad campaigns in real time, combining
  several specialized generative models (motion, faces, lighting) into one cut.
- Delivery model is managed production, not self-serve software: brands get a finished
  brand or performance video in roughly 72 hours.
- Named customers include Hims, Kalshi, Replit, Brex, Whop and Reforge. Their Kalshi
  "Election Day" spot passed 1M views; their videos average 400K+ organic views.
